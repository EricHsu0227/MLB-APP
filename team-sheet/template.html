How the numbers are made

Built-in seasons (★) are complete and work offline. Counting stats and L/R splits are MLB's official numbers. Plate discipline, batted ball, pitch type and pitch values come from every regular-season pitch in Statcast. wOBA weights, the wOBA scale, the FIP constant and park factors are calculated from that season's own data with FanGraphs' published method, so they land close to FanGraphs' values but not exactly on them.

Other seasons load MLB's official stats live (internet needed) and use standard wOBA weights, with no park factors.

Not included: FanGraphs-only stats (WAR, Off, Def, BsR, UZR, Stuff+) and FanGraphs' Soft/Med/Hard%, which come from private data. Plate discipline uses the Statcast strike zone. ERA, W/L, R, ER, SB and CS don't exist as L/R splits.
